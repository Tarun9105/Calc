## Full Description
SmartCalc is a comprehensive scientific calculator designed to match the familiarity of modern native calculators while adding powerful features for everyday math, students, and engineers.

### Features:
- **Basic Arithmetic**: Fast, responsive portrait keypad for everyday calculations.
- **Scientific Mode**: Rotate to landscape to reveal advanced functions like trigonometry, logarithms, powers, and factorials.
- **History & Memory**: Never lose track of a calculation. Save values to memory and recall your past calculation history instantly.
- **Customization & Accessibility**: Switch between light and dark themes, adjust text size, customize decimal precision, and toggle haptic feedback to suit your preference.
- **Offline First**: Works 100% offline with zero unnecessary permissions.

Whether you're calculating a tip or solving complex equations, SmartCalc delivers a fast, error-free experience.
